# TikTok Selfie-Style Fashion Prompts

This collection covers casual, self-filmed fashion video prompts. The garment/product references define product truth; the prompt should not invent construction details or transfer unrelated clothing from a motion reference.

## Reusable decisions

- Choose the frame for the business goal: a close opening can feel like a real phone recording, while the main display section must keep the full garment visible when that is the selling point.
- Describe the phone's height, angle, lens feel, and whether it remains fixed. If framing changes, state whether the subject walks or the camera moves.
- Keep the action count small and natural. For narrow skirts or long dresses, specify short steps, foot placement, and whether the wearer remains in one spot during a turn.
- Lock identity, garment construction, accessories, which hand holds each item, and the room layout to the image references.
- Keep a continuous take only when the motion is feasible. If a turn morphs the garment, test explicit hard cuts as a separate version rather than quietly mixing both strategies.
- Label prompts as pending until a generated sample is reviewed. Save specific failure evidence, such as a slit appearing, accessory switching hands, or a mirror creating a duplicate person.
